# Ozone Prompt Pro (12)

🧠 MASTER WEBSITE GENERATION PROMPT — CABINET OZONE CONSEIL

Objective:
Create a production-ready, professional, SEO-optimized, and AI-search-optimized website for Cabinet Ozone Conseil, a Cabinet Géomètre-Expert located in BP 113, Godomey, Benin. The website must be visually elegant, engaging, mobile responsive, and conversion-oriented, reflecting professionalism and technical expertise in geomatics, surveying, and land management.

📍 Business Details

Name: Cabinet Ozone Conseil

Type: Cabinet Géomètre-Expert (Land Surveying & Consulting)

Address: BP 113, Godomey, Benin

Phone: 01 97 88 90 01

WhatsApp: +229 97 88 90 01

Languages: French (main), English (secondary for international audience)

Primary CTA: “Parlez à un Expert Maintenant” / “Chat with an Expert Now”

🎯 Website Goals

Build trust and credibility through professional design and clear value propositions.

Highlight services with strong visuals, modular service pages, and CTAs.

Encourage visitors to contact via phone, WhatsApp, or integrated chat.

Rank highly for local and professional search terms in Benin and West Africa.

Integrate testimonials, achievements, partners, and real project showcases.

🖥️ Website Structure
1. Hero Section (Homepage)

Full-width background (surveyors in action, drone topography, or precision mapping).

Tagline: “Cabinet Ozone Conseil – L’Expertise Géomatique au Service du Territoire”

Subtext: “Précision, Fiabilité, et Innovation pour vos projets fonciers et topographiques.”

CTAs:

“Demandez un devis” (Request a Quote)

“Chattez avec un Expert Maintenant” (floating button to WhatsApp/chat)

Trust badges (e.g., “Membre de l’Ordre des Géomètres-Experts”, “+10 ans d’expérience”, “100+ projets réussis”).

2. About Section

Brief history of the firm.

Mission & values (Integrity, Accuracy, Innovation, Sustainability).

Team photo or animated portrait slider with key experts.

Quote: “Notre mission : garantir la précision qui bâtit la confiance.”

3. Services Section (Cards leading to modular detail pages)

Each service should have:

A clean card with icon/illustration.

Short description + “En savoir plus” link leading to a dedicated service page.

Main Services:

Topographie & Cartographie – surveys, mapping, digital terrain modeling.

Bornage et Délimitation Foncière – property boundaries, legal land measurements.

Levés par Drone & Photogrammétrie – modern aerial mapping solutions.

Aménagement du Territoire & Urbanisme – spatial planning and development.

Études Techniques & VRD (Voirie Réseaux Divers) – infrastructure & civil works.

Conseil & Expertise Foncière – legal, technical, and strategic advisory.

✅ On each service page:

Hero banner for that service.

Description (problem → solution → method).

Real project example or client testimonial.

CTA: “Chattez avec un Expert Maintenant” (sticky button).

4. Achievements / Projects

Dynamic grid or timeline view.

Showcase completed and ongoing projects with metrics like:

“+500 hectares mesurés”

“20 communes desservies”

“100+ clients satisfaits”

Include geospatial visuals (map pins, project photos, before/after imagery).

5. Partners & Clients

Display partner logos (construction firms, government agencies, real estate developers).

Add client testimonials carousel (with star ratings).

Include a “Join Our Partner Network” CTA for B2B outreach.

6. Contact Section

Contact form (Name, Email, Project Type, Message).

Embedded Google Map showing office location.

Quick contact buttons:

Call now

WhatsApp chat

Request a quote

Display business hours and office address clearly.

7. Footer

Quick navigation links (Home, Services, About, Projects, Contact).

Social media icons (LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp).

Copyright line: “© 2025 Cabinet Ozone Conseil. Tous droits réservés.”

SEO microdata (schema.org for Local Business).

🔍 SEO & AI Search Optimization

Meta title: “Cabinet Ozone Conseil | Géomètre-Expert à Godomey, Benin”

Meta description: “Experts en topographie, bornage, cartographie, et aménagement du territoire. Contactez Cabinet Ozone Conseil pour des services géomatiques précis et fiables.”

Keywords: géomètre expert bénin, topographie Godomey, cabinet géomètre, bornage, cartographie drone.

Structured data (schema.org: LocalBusiness, Service, Review).

Image alt texts for all visuals.

Fast-loading, mobile-first layout.

AI-search optimization: natural language content, FAQs (“Comment choisir un géomètre-expert ?”, etc.).

🧩 Bonus: Engagement & Conversion Features

Floating “Chat with an Expert Now” button linked to WhatsApp API.

Lead capture pop-up: “Obtenez votre devis gratuit en 24h.”

Sticky CTA bar on mobile view.

Newsletter signup for updates or educational blog posts (“Guide du bornage foncier au Bénin”).

Animated counters for achievements.

Light animations (Framer Motion style or Tailwind transitions).

💡 Design Direction

Color palette: professional blues, whites, and gold accents (trust + precision).

Typography: Sans-serif modern font (e.g., Poppins / Inter).

Style: clean grid layout, subtle shadows, rounded cards, high legibility.

Imagery: drone shots, surveying equipment, urban planning visuals.

🛠️ Technical Stack (if applicable)

Framework: Next.js / React / Tailwind CSS

Deployment: Vercel / Netlify

Analytics: Google Analytics 4 + Meta Pixel

Chat Integration: WhatsApp API or Tawk.to widget
